Restaurant Services

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

Hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort all interact, and an undersized or failing system shows up first as a sweltering kitchen and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. Our technicians balance makeup air against hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports your landlord and auditors can review.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep disruption to a minimum so the kitchen keeps running and the dining room stays comfortable.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, check electrical connections and disconnects, document the refrigerant charge, protect against condensate overflow, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

When a commercial HVAC system fails, the clock starts immediately. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling fast, throughout Dunwoody and the surrounding area.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed compressor, a burned-out condenser fan motor, a refrigerant leak, an ignition or gas valve fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a frozen coil, or a control board stuck in lockout. Whatever it is, we find it fast, restore heating or cooling, and record every step for your service records. Afterward, we put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again.
